MTG Data.com Rating Guide About

About This Project

Welcome! This research website is dedicated to collecting player opinions on Magic: The Gathering cards, specifically focusing on their power levels and playability. For detailed guidelines on how to provide ratings, please visit our Rating Guide.

On this page, you'll find information about how your data is managed, it's intended use, prize incentives for participants, and details about the researcher behind this project.

How We Handle Your Data

Your privacy matters to us. All submitted data remains completely anonymous. You may optionally provide an email address, but this will only be used to save your progress or contact you regarding prize winnings. Email addresses will never be shared or published. All collected responses will be anonymized before any analysis or reporting.

Purpose of the Data

The data gathered here supports a college research project at the University of Washington. It will serve as a benchmark to evaluate how accurately reasoning-based large language models (LLMs) can quantify card power and playability. Additionally, the data may be used to train machine learning models for similar predictions. Potential outcomes from this project include:

  • Class reports
  • Academic research papers
  • A Magic card power-level prediction bot
